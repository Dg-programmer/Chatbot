Open BABY on a webpage

Click the floating pet or the BABY toolbar icon. Pages that were open when you installed or updated the extension need one reload. Drag the pet to place it anywhere in the viewport.

By default, the browser pet takes over on the active webpage. Desktop BABY returns in other apps and on tabs where the extension is unavailable.

Use the microphone to ask a question, the speaker to hear replies, and the paperclip to include files.

Browser settings, extension stores, and other protected pages prevent the pet from appearing.
