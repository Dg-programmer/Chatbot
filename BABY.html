    BABY Browser Pet

    Move BABY around a webpage, then click to chat, speak, or attach a file.

    1. Download and unzip the extension.
    2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder named extension.
    4. Keep local BABY open and connected. Reload the webpage, then click the pet.
    5. Pair the extension with your hosted BABY below. Reload the webpage, then click the pet.

    When you send a question, BABY sends readable page text and attached files to your connected model.

    Connect the extension

    Open the pet’s Connection settings and paste this address and a one-time code.
